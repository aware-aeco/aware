# UK steel drawing / detailing standards (what governs what)

| Standard | Governs | Free / Paid |
|---|---|---|
| **BCSA 45/07** ("Orange Book") | allocation of design responsibilities + drawing-type definitions + checklists | **Free** |
| **NSSS** — National Structural Steelwork Specification (BCSA/SCI; 7th Ed., 2020) | the default UK specification: materials, information exchange, workmanship, welding, bolting, fabrication & erection accuracy, protective treatment, QM (aligned to EN 1090-2, EXC2 + an EXC3 annex) | Paid |
| **BS EN 1090-1 / -2** | CE/UKCA conformity assessment / technical requirements for execution (defines EXC1–EXC4) | Paid |
| **BS EN ISO 2553** | **weld symbols** — symbolic representation of welded joints on drawings (UK uses **System A**) | Paid |
| **BS 8888** | UK technical product documentation / engineering-drawing conventions (incl. fasteners) | Paid |
| **BS EN 1993-1-8** + UK NA | design of connections (the calc basis) | Paid |
| **SCI P358** ("Green Book") | UK tabulated connection resistances — the detailer's working reference | **Free PDF** |

**Weld symbols & bolt representation:** the symbol library is the *content* of **BS EN ISO 2553** (welds; **System A** is the UK convention) and **BS 8888** (general drafting, incl. fasteners) — both paid. Store *which standard governs* and pull the actual symbol shapes from the licensed standard — never from memory or third-party blogs.
