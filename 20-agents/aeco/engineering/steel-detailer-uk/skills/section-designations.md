# Section types & naming (UK)

**Open sections:** **UB** (universal beam), **UC** (universal column), **UBP** (universal bearing pile), **PFC** (parallel flange channel). Tata's CE-marked **Advance®** range names these **UKB / UKC / UKBP / UKPFC** (tee = **UKT**, angle = **UKA**). Note: "UB"/"UC" are common shorthand, **not** formal BS designations.

**Angles:** equal & unequal leg (L / UKA). **Hollow sections:** **SHS / RHS / CHS** (square / rectangular / circular) — Celsius® (hot-finished, EN 10210), Hybox® (cold-formed, EN 10219).

**Naming convention:**
- **UB / UC / PFC** = serial **depth (mm) × flange width (mm) × mass per metre (kg/m)** — e.g. **UB 457×191×67**, **UC 254×254×73**, **PFC 230×90×32**.
- **Angles** = **leg × leg × thickness (mm)** — e.g. 120×120×8.

**Governing dimension standards:** open sections to **EN 10365:2017** (which superseded the section-dimension role of BS 4-1); angles to **BS EN 10056**; hollow sections to **BS EN 10210 / 10219**.

**Free property data:** the SCI **"Blue Book" (P363)** and the online **eBlueBook** (steelforlifebluebook.co.uk) give section properties + member/connection resistances (grades S275/S355; the eBlueBook adds S460) — the source to drive a section lookup without hardcoding tables.
