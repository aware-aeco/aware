# Steel grades (UK / Eurocode)

**Designation:** `S` + number = the nominal yield strength **fy** (N/mm²) for the *thinnest* thickness range (S = structural). E.g. **S355** = minimum yield 355 N/mm² for t ≤ 16 mm. Common grades: S235, **S275**, **S355**, S460 (EN 10025-2).

**fy reduces with thickness** (EN 10025-2):

| Grade | t ≤ 16 | ≤ 40 | ≤ 63 | ≤ 80 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S275** | 275 | 265 | 255 | 245 |
| **S355** | 355 | 345 | 335 | 325 |

(fu also reduces with thickness; the nominal **fu = 410 N/mm² (S275) / 470 N/mm² (S355)** used for design up to 100 mm.)

**Subgrades — Charpy V-notch impact toughness:** **JR** = 27 J at +20 °C; **J0** = 27 J at 0 °C; **J2** = 27 J at −20 °C; **K2** = **40 J at −20 °C**.

**Hollow sections:** **EN 10210** (hot-finished) / **EN 10219** (cold-formed) — e.g. **S355J2H** ("H" = hollow section; also S355K2H). Tata ranges: Celsius® (hot-finished), Hybox® (cold-formed).

**Bolts:** property classes **4.6 / 8.8** (non-preloaded, BS EN 15048) and **8.8 / 10.9** (preloaded, BS EN 14399 — System HR = 8.8/10.9, System HRC = 10.9).
