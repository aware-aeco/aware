# Connection design responsibility (UK practice)

- In typical UK practice the **design engineer specifies the connection forces / reactions** (on the GA drawings), and the **steelwork contractor designs — selects and verifies — the connections** to carry them. *"Connection selection and verification is generally the responsibility of the steelwork contractor who will choose the connection type to suit the fabrication workload, economy and temporary stability during erection."* (steelconstruction.info "Simple connections")
- **Exception:** if **semi-continuous** design is used, "both the beam and its connections must be selected and verified by the frame designer."
- **Mechanism:** the GA carries "the forces to be developed in their connections … necessary for the design of the connections" (BCSA 45/07 §2.1). BCSA 45/07 allocates **who does what design, when, and how information flows** via its eight-area Checklists — and is explicit that it does **not** assign legal liability, "simply the practicalities of who does what design."
- **Calc basis:** connection design follows **BS EN 1993-1-8** (+ UK NA), with UK tabulated capacities in **SCI P358** (the Green Book) — see this agent's connection-rule skills. The formal NSSS definition of "a connection" is **NSSS wording (paid)** — cite the NSSS as governing, do not quote it from memory.
