# The UK steel drawing chain (who produces what)

UK steel documentation flows through defined stages (BCSA Publication **45/07** "Allocation of Design Responsibilities in Constructional Steelwork", §2.1):

- **Structural Engineering Drawings** — "drawings indicating the outline structural requirements for the Project" (the design team).
- **Steelwork General Arrangement (GA) Drawings** — "fully dimensioned drawings showing the location of all members together with their size and material grades, **the forces to be developed in their connections**, any cambers and eccentricities and other information necessary for the design of the connections and completion of Fabrication and Erection Drawings" (the consulting / design engineer).
- **Fabrication Drawings** — "drawings showing all necessary information required for the Steelwork fabrication element of the Project" (the steelwork contractor / detailer).
- **Erection Drawings** — "showing details amplifying the information given in the erection method statement … and details of any temporary Steelwork."

The **Steelwork Contractor** undertakes "any or all of the design, detailing, fabrication and erection of the Steelwork." Modern UK practice is model-based: one 3D fabrication model (e.g. Tekla) generates the fabrication, assembly and erection drawings plus CNC data.
