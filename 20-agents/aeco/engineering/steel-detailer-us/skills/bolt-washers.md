# Bolt washers (RCSC)

Washers are generally **not required** for snug-tightened Group 120/144/150 joints, **except**:

- **Sloping surface (§6.1.1):** when the outer face of the joint slopes **> 1:20** from a plane normal to the bolt axis, use an **ASTM F436 beveled washer** to compensate for the lack of parallelism.
- **Slotted / oversized hole in an outer ply (§6.1.2, §6.2.6 + Table 6.1):** a washer (ASTM F436, or for slots a 5/16-in.-thick plate washer, or per Table 6.1) must **completely cover the hole**.

For **pretensioned & slip-critical** joints, additionally:

- **§6.2.1:** Group 144/150 bolts in connected material with **Fy < 40 ksi** → ASTM F436 washers under **both head and nut** (except not required under the round head of an F3125 Gr. F2280 or F3148 Gr. 144 bolt).
- **§6.2.2 / §6.2.3 / §6.2.4:** calibrated-wrench, twist-off-type (TC), and combined methods → ASTM F436 washer under the **turned element (nut)**.
- **§6.2.5:** DTI placed under the turned element → an ASTM F436 washer between the turned element and the DTI.
