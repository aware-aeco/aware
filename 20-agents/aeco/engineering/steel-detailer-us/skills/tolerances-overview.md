# Tolerances overview (AISC)

AISC 303 recognises three tolerance regimes and is largely a **pointer** to where each is defined:

- **Mill tolerances** — set by **ASTM A6/A6M** (rolled shapes and plate) and the applicable ASTM standards for HSS / pipe. (§5.1.2) More-restrictive mill tolerances must be specified in the contract documents.
- **Fabrication tolerances** — AISC 303's own fabrication provisions, plus **AISC 360 / the AISC Manual / the RCSC Specification / AWS D1.1** as incorporated by the contract documents. (303-22 §6 area)
- **Erection tolerances** — AISC 303's erection provisions (plumbness, elevation, alignment), with owner acceptance. (303-22 §7 area)
- **Accumulation rule:** *"The accumulation of mill tolerances and fabrication tolerances shall not cause the erection tolerances to be exceeded."* (§7.12)
- Absence of a stated special tolerance does **not** mean the tolerance is zero; special / tighter tolerances must be called out in the contract documents (§1.11; AISC 360 §A4.1).

## Source

- **AISC 303-22 §1.11, §5.1.2, §6 (fabrication), §7 incl. §7.12 (erection + accumulation).** Verified via the AISC 303-27 public-review draft + a 303-16 verbatim source. *Numbering note:* the 303-27 draft consolidates all tolerances into a new §11, but in **303-22 they are inline at §5 / §6 / §7** (cited here). The full numeric tolerance tables live in the standard — this skill is pointer-level. Free at aisc.org/standards.
