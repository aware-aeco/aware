# Drawing types & roles (AISC Code of Standard Practice)

AISC 303 defines three document families:

- **Design documents / design drawings** — prepared by / for the **owner's designated representative for design (ODRD; the EOR)**. *"Graphic and pictorial portions of the contract documents showing the design, location, and dimensions of the work"* (plans, elevations, sections, details, schedules, notes). When *issued for construction* they must be based on a completed design and carry the building-code + **AISC 360 §A4** information, shop-painting/surface-prep requirements, AESS category, and leveling-plate info. (§3.1)
- **Approval documents** = the fabricator-produced **shop (detail) drawings + erection drawings (+ embedment drawings)**. *Shop drawings* = "drawings of the individual structural steel shipping pieces that are to be produced in the fabrication shop." (§4.2.1)
- **Erection drawings** = "field-installation or member-placement drawings showing the location and attachment of the individual structural steel shipping pieces."

**Who does what:** the **ODRD issues the design documents** (§3); the **fabricator produces the approval documents** — transferring the contract-document information into accurate, dimensioned, field-fit-up-ready detail (also AISC 360 §M1); the **erector** erects.
