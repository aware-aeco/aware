# Shop / erection drawing approval (AISC 303)

- The fabricator submits the **approval documents** to the ODRD and ODRC; they are returned **within 14 calendar days**, individually annotated **"approved"** or **"approved subject to corrections noted."** (§4.4)
- **What approval means** (§4.4.1): (a) confirmation the fabricator **correctly interpreted the contract documents**; (b) confirmation the ODRD has **reviewed and approved the connection details** (per §3.2.3); (c) **release** for the fabricator to begin fabrication using the approved submittals.
- **What approval does NOT do** (§4.4.1): *"Such approval shall not relieve the fabricator of the responsibility for either the accuracy of the detailed dimensions in the approval documents or the fit-up of parts that are to be assembled in the field."* Dimensional accuracy and field fit-up remain the fabricator's responsibility.
- The 14 days is the fabricator's "portal-to-portal" time; a longer period (e.g. when substantiating connection information is included) should be specified in the contract documents. The owner must first furnish design documents **released for construction** (§4.1); changes made on RFIs or annotated approval documents constitute the owner's authorization that they are released for construction (§4.4.2).
