# Bolt spacing — minimum & maximum (AISC)

**Minimum spacing between bolt centers = 2⅔·d** (≈ 2.67·d), where *d* = nominal bolt diameter. The **clear distance** between holes (or slots) must also be **≥ d**. **3·d is preferred.** — AISC 360-22 §J3.4

**Maximum spacing** (elements in continuous contact — sealing / stitch):
- Painted members, or unpainted members not subject to atmospheric corrosion: ≤ **24·t** or **12 in. (300 mm)**, whichever is less.
- Unpainted weathering steel subject to atmospheric corrosion: ≤ **14·t** or **7 in. (180 mm)**.

(*t* = thickness of the thinner connected part.) — AISC 360-22 §J3.6

## How to apply

- Use 3·d when you have room; 2⅔·d is the hard floor. Also check **clear distance ≥ d**, which can govern for large-diameter or closely-packed holes.
- Maximum-spacing limits keep faying surfaces in contact (resist moisture ingress / buckling between fasteners) — they apply to sealing/stitch, not to the strength bolt group.

## Source

- Minimum: **AISC 360-22 §J3.4.** *"The distance between centers of standard, oversized, or slotted holes shall not be less than 2⅔ times the nominal diameter, d, of the fastener … the clear distance between bolt holes or slots shall not be less than d."* (User note: a spacing of 3d is preferred.)
- Maximum: **AISC 360-22 §J3.6.**
- Verify free at aisc.org/standards. *(These are the 360-22 section numbers; 360-16 numbered them §J3.3 / §J3.5.)*
