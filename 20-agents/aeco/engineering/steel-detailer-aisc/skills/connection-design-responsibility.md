# Connection design responsibility — the AISC 303 options

For **each connection**, the ODRD (EOR) must designate one of three options (**§3.2.3**):

- **Option 1** — the complete connection design is **shown in the structural design documents** (the EOR designs it).
- **Option 2** — the connection is to be **selected or completed by a steel detailer**, using reference information the EOR provides in the design documents.
- **Option 3** — the connection is to be **designed by a licensed engineer working for the fabricator**. This requires **substantiating connection information**: the fabricator submits representative samples to the ODRD (who confirms in writing), and the fabricator's licensed engineer confirms in writing that the approval documents properly incorporate the connection designs (this does **not** replace the ODRD's approval per §4.4).

When **Option 2 or 3** is specified, the EOR must provide: conceptual/representative connection details for the order-of-magnitude forces; any restrictions on connection types; the loads (shears, moments, axial and transfer forces); whether they are **service-level or factored**; whether **LRFD or ASD** applies; and what substantiating information is required.

The **ODRD is the final authority** in any disagreement over connection design (§4.4).
